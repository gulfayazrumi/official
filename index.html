    Before you hire me.

    How long does a project take?

    It depends on size. A simple business site can take days, while a large store takes longer. After our first conversation I give you a clear timeline.

    Can you fix or speed up my existing site?

    Yes. I check what is slowing it down, show you, and fix the biggest issues first.

    Shopify or WordPress: which one do I need?

    Shopify is best when selling products is your main goal. WordPress suits service businesses and content-heavy sites. WooCommerce adds a shop to WordPress. I'll recommend one based on your goals.

    Can I edit the site myself?

    Yes. I set sites up so you can change text, images and products without code, and I show you how.
